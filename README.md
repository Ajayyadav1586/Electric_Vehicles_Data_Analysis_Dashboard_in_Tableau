# 🚗 Electric_Vehicles_Data_Analysis_Dashboard_in_Tablea
A comprehensive data analysis and interactive visualization project focused on electric vehicle (EV) adoption, market share by manufacturer, vehicle range metrics, and Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility trends built using **Tableau** and **Python**.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze the growth, distribution, and market performance of Electric Vehicles (EVs) using historical registration data. The insights generated aim to help automakers, policymakers, and infrastructure planners understand:
* Overall EV adoption growth across model years.
* Dominant market players (Makes & Models) driving adoption.
* Distribution between Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs).
* Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility compliance across registered fleets.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Questions Addressed

### 📈 Core Metrics (KPI Cards)
1. **Total Vehicles**: **150,413** registered electric vehicles.
2. **Average Electric Range**: **67.83 Miles** across the dataset.
3. **Total BEVs**: **116,745** (~**77.6%** of total EV adoption).
4. **Total PHEVs**: **33,668** (~**22.4%** of total EV adoption).

### ❓ Analytical Questions Addressed
* **Growth Trend**: How has EV adoption evolved year-over-year from 2011 to 2024?
* **Geographic Distribution**: Which states and cities account for the highest concentration of EVs?
* **Market Share**: Which manufacturers (Makes) dominate total EV registrations?
* **Model Leadership**: What are the top-performing EV models in terms of total units sold?
* **Policy Compliance**: What proportion of vehicles qualify for CAFV incentives vs. low battery range disqualifications?

---

## 💡 Key Project Insights

1. **Market Dominance by Tesla**:
   * Tesla commands **52.7%** of the total EV market share with **68,939** vehicles.
   * Top models including **Tesla Model Y** and **Tesla Model 3** lead overall individual model sales.
   * Nissan (**10.32%**) and Chevrolet (**9.19%**) represent the primary non-Tesla competitors.

2. **Vehicle Type Preference**:
   * Pure Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) heavily outperform Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) in consumer adoption, representing over **77%** of all registrations.

3. **Adoption Growth Curve**:
   * EV adoption experienced exponential growth between **2020 (11.3K)** and **2023 (37.1K)**, reaching a peak registration volume in 2023 before leveling out in 2024 data collections.

4. **Geographic Concentration**:
   * The vast majority of registered vehicles are heavily concentrated within Washington State (WA), highlighting regional policy and infrastructure influence on adoption rates.

5. **CAFV Eligibility Breakdown**:
   * Approximately **62,887** vehicles are fully eligible for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle incentives, while a significant chunk remaining is marked pending/research required or ineligible due to low battery range.
